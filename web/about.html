About IdentityZA

Building high-performance, privacy-first identity validation tools and educational documentation for South Africa.

Our Mission & Origin

IdentityZA began as an open-source systems programming initiative created in modern C++ to solve a critical, everyday technical challenge in South Africa: how to reliably, accurately, and securely validate 13-digit South African National Identity numbers without relying on sluggish external APIs or exposing citizen identity numbers to untrusted remote networks.

In South Africa, National ID numbers are the universal backbone of digital identity, used in banking, retail accounts, cell phone contracts (RICA), employment verification, and government registrations. However, many existing web forms and verification tools transmit sensitive ID numbers across third-party backend servers, creating unnecessary cybersecurity vulnerabilities and privacy risks under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

The Architecture: Privacy-First Engineering

IdentityZA is built with a dual-tier architecture:

1. High-Performance C++ Core

A native C++ validation suite designed for automated backend batch processing, enterprise KYC pipelines, and high-frequency verification with zero external dependencies and sub-microsecond execution times.

2. Zero-Transmission Web Engine

A lightweight, browser-based web application that executes the complete Luhn checksum, date verification, and demographic extraction purely on the client's device using vanilla JavaScript and modern CSS standards.

Who We Serve

  • Everyday Citizens: Individuals who want to verify the validity of their ID number, check whether their checksum matches official DHA specifications, or calculate their exact age and biographical data.
  • Software Developers & Fintechs: Programmers building onboarding funnels, e-commerce checkouts, and customer sign-up forms who require reference implementations, mathematical documentation, and algorithmic guides for the South African Luhn variation.
  • Human Resources & Compliance Officers: Teams needing an instant, zero-cost, private method to sanity-check prospective employee identification documents before proceeding to formal biometric screening.

Open Source & Community

We believe in the power of open-source software to elevate security standards across the South African digital economy. The entire source code for IdentityZA—including the command-line C++ tool and the web application—is hosted publicly on GitHub and distributed under the permissive MIT License.

Developers are encouraged to review the code, fork the repository, suggest algorithmic improvements, or integrate the validation logic into their own internal systems.
