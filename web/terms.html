2. Nature of the Service: Educational & Structural Verification Only

IdentityZA is an algorithmic verification tool and educational resource designed to inspect the structural formatting, calendar date integrity, and Luhn Modulus 10 checksum of 13-digit South African National Identity numbers.

⚠️ Crucial Operational Notice

IdentityZA does NOT perform official government biometric identity verification. Algorithmic validity indicates solely that an ID number complies with the mathematical formula established by South African standards. It does not verify whether the individual is living or deceased, confirm marital status, validate legal names, or guarantee that an identity document was lawfully issued by the Department of Home Affairs.
