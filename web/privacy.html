Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction & Our Core Privacy Commitment

At IdentityZA ("we", "our", or "us"), privacy is not an afterthought—it is the foundational architectural principle of our application. IdentityZA was designed and engineered specifically to allow South Africans, software engineers, and businesses to validate and decode South African National Identity numbers without having to trust any third party with sensitive personal data.

🔒 Zero-Server Transmission Architecture

Your 13-digit South African ID number is processed 100% locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not transmit, collect, store, log, or record any ID numbers or demographic information on our servers or databases.

2. Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) Compliance

In accordance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (Act No. 4 of 2013, "POPIA"):

  • No Personal Data Processing by Our Servers: Because all algorithms, Luhn checksum checks, date-of-birth parsing, and age calculations occur exclusively within your device's memory, IdentityZA does not act as an "operator" or "responsible party" storing your identity number.
  • Local Storage Retention: For user convenience during active sessions, recent verification queries are optionally cached in your own browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be permanently wiped at any moment by clicking the "Clear" button in the history card or clearing your browser cache.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, lease, monetize, or disclose personal data, identity numbers, or search queries to third-party data brokers, marketing agencies, or credit bureaus.
